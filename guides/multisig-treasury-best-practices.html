Multi-Sig Treasury Best Practices for Crypto Teams

Why multi-sig

A multi-sig wallet means no single compromised key drains the treasury. For any team holding meaningful USDC, it's the difference between an incident and a catastrophe.

Structure that actually works

Signers: 3-of-5 or 2-of-3 — enough redundancy, not too much friction. Mix custody types (one hardware signer, one software, one founder) so no single attack vector controls the wallet.

Keys: seed phrases on paper/metal, never digital. One signer per human; no shared keys.

Workflow: every payout needs (1) an approved proposal or invoice, (2) transaction details logged before signing, (3) two independent signers verifying the recipient address, (4) post-tx logging within 24h.
