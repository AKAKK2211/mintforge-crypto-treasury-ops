Crypto Business Tax Records: The Documentation Framework

The principle: you can't file what you can't reconstruct

A business that logs every transaction within 24 hours and closes monthly is tax-ready by default. The documentation framework is simple and consistent:

1. Transaction logs — every wallet, every year, in one format (date, hash, counterparty, amount, USD value, category).

2. Wallet inventory — your mapping of addresses to purposes (the chain is public; your labels are the record).

3. Monthly closes — reconciliation evidence that balances tied out.

4. Invoices & payments — customer payments matched to delivered value (the same evidence a revenue ledger needs).

5. Method note — which accounting method you use for crypto (e.g., FIFO vs average cost) — confirm treatment with your accountant.

Not tax strategy, not entity advice, not jurisdiction-specific rules.
