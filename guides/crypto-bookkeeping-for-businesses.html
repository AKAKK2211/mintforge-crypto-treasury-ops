Crypto Bookkeeping for Businesses: The 2026 Practical Guide

The #1 mistake: treating wallet transfers as income

Moving USDC from your hot wallet to your cold wallet is not revenue. It's not an expense. It's an internal transfer — and mislabeling it is the most common bookkeeping error in crypto businesses. Every transfer between wallets you control goes in its own category, and it never touches your P&L.

The 3 habits that keep books clean

1. Log within 24h. Every transaction: date, tx hash, counterparty, purpose, amount, USD value at the time, category. Same-day logging takes 2 minutes and saves hours at month-end.

2. Map your wallets. One inventory line per wallet (hot, cold, receiving) with purpose and owner. Chain data is public — YOUR mapping of addresses to purposes is the record that matters.

3. Reconcile monthly. Pull each wallet's export from the chain explorer, match every hash to your log, investigate unmatched within 48h, then close the month. Never carry unknown balances.
