USDC Accounting: Tracking Stablecoin Transactions Correctly

Precision matters: USDC has 6 decimals

USDC amounts on-chain are integer values in 6-decimal units (1 USDC = 1,000,000 units). When you export transactions, amounts like "500000000" mean $500.00. Get this conversion right or every report is off by a factor of a million.

Stablecoin ≠ free of bookkeeping

USDC may be "stable," but your books still need: payment vs transfer classification, fee/gas handling (is the network fee an expense or part of the transaction?), USD value at the time of each transaction, and invoice matching (which customer payment paid which invoice).

The monthly close

1. Export every wallet from the chain explorer → 2. Match every hash to your log → 3. Verify all customer payments landed (the revenue check) → 4. Confirm internal transfers aren't counted as income → 5. Record USD values at transaction time → 6. File the month read-only.
